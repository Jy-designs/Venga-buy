    Buy FAQ

    Here is additional information to answer some of the common questions about buying crypto and how to benefit from it with Venga.

    Buying crypto gives you direct ownership of digital assets like Bitcoin and Ethereum, which you can hold, sell or swap whenever you want in the Venga app. Crypto prices are volatile and you could lose money, so only invest what you can afford to lose.

    You buy crypto by bank transfer: transfer euros to the IBAN shown in the app and we execute your buy order once they arrive. If an order can’t be executed, the euros go back to the account they came from.

    More than 80 crypto assets, including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Chainlink (LINK) and Solana (SOL). Open the app to see the full list.

    Venga is authorised by Spain’s CNMV under MiCA. Your crypto is kept separate from Venga’s own assets, most of it in cold storage, and you see every fee before you confirm an order.

    Venga operates in the EU under its MiCA authorisation. Check the app or our Help Center to see if Venga is available where you live.
